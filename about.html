THE GENESIS

From a workshop to your world.

It began in 1984, in a small, aromatic workshop in the heart of Palakkad. Our founder, driven by a passion for the perfect "crunch," started experimenting with local varieties of bananas and ancient spice blends.

"The secret isn't just in the recipe, but in the hands that press the dough and the heart that waits for the perfect gold."

At AGAN'S, we believe that time is the most important ingredient. Every bag carries forty years of refinement, tradition, and uncompromising Kerala spirit.
